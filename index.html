About Me

I am a physicist and astronomer interested in planetary science, celestial mechanics and exoplanetary systems. My research combines analytical methods, numerical simulations, and observational techniques to study the dynamics, architecture, and long-term evolution of planetary systems.

My current research interests include mean-motion resonances, co-orbital dynamics, transit timing variations (TTVs), and photometric follow-up observations of exoplanets.
